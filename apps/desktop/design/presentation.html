ARIA / DESKTOP DESIGN 03 · IDENTITY

Meet the Signal A.

A recognisable initial, crossed by a small voice signal. A stronger silhouette at desktop-icon sizes.

Primary horizontal lockup

Reversed on forest

One mark. Useful sizes.

16 / 24 / 32 / 64 px
Use a simplified A without the pulse at 16 px.

Its own identity

No coat of arms, no redrawn NITDA emblem.
The supplied reference informs colour only.

Keep it clean

Clear space: ¼ symbol width.
Single ink, white, or forest. No glow on the logo.
